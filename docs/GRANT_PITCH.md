## What TURBO is

A free-to-enter, 52-week program that takes people in rural Guerrero from no
development experience to **a contract they deployed themselves to Monad mainnet
from their own wallet.**

Free to enter is a recent change and a deliberate one. The program previously
charged monthly membership, which selected for people with spare cash rather than
people who ship. In a region where that filter excludes almost everyone, it was
working against the goal.

## Why the metric is honest

Graduation is one thing, and it is verifiable by anyone without trusting me:

> a contract deployed to Monad mainnet, from a wallet the student controls,
> that they can explain

No self-reported completions, no attendance records, no dashboard only I can see.
Every graduate is a mainnet address with deploy history. Audit the whole cohort
from a block explorer.

That also means this proposal can be judged on delivery rather than on promises —
count the deploys at the end and pay accordingly if you prefer milestones.
